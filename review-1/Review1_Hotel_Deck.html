Problem Identification

Why this system is needed

Existing Operational Points

  • Difficulty tracking real-time room availability.
  • High probability of double-booking during peak periods.
  • Inconsistent guest and reservation record keeping.
  • Missed or duplicated in-stay service charges.
  • Lack of real-time visibility into housekeeping status.
  • Manual billing calculations leading to invoice errors.

Problem Statement

"A centralized, automated database system is required to coordinate room reservations, stay management, guest services, housekeeping, billing, and payment processing — while maintaining data consistency and operational speed."

Data Model

Main Database Entities

Inventory & pricing
Room Type
Category defining capacity and nightly rate tier.
Room
Physical room with number, type, and live status.
Rate Plan
Pricing plan tied to a room type.
Guest activity
Guest
Contact details and identity for bookings.
Reservation
Planned booking — dates, guests, status.
Stay
Actual check-in / check-out against a reservation.
In-stay services
Housekeeping Task
Cleaning or maintenance job for a room.
Service Request
In-stay guest request for a service.
Service Charge
Billable amount for a fulfilled request.
Billing
Invoice
Subtotal, tax, and total for a stay.
Payment
Amount, method, and status against an invoice.
